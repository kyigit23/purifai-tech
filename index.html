The problem

Farms are pumping groundwater faster than it refills.

Most of it comes from wells nobody measures, and much of it is lost to flood irrigation. The fix exists and the state already pays for most of it. It just isn't reaching the right farms.

2.1bn m³

pumped every year beyond what the Konya basin can refill

now under an official groundwater restriction in Türkiye

of irrigated land moved to modern irrigation with grants since 2007

How it works

From a satellite pass to a new irrigation system.

01

Detect

Satellite imagery and AI estimate how much water each field really uses, and flag the ones pumping far more than their crop needs.

02

Match

We send each flagged farm to a local drip or solar-pump dealer, with the grant and 0% loan that cover most of the cost.

Farmer Local dealer Grant up to 70% 0% loan
03

Verify

After installation we check the same field from space and report the water saved to banks and agencies.

Self-learning model

A model that gets sharper with every well.

Satellites see the whole basin. Partner wells tell us exactly how much was pumped. Each measured well recalibrates the model for the fields around it.

  • Basin-wide coverageEvery irrigated field, refreshed every few days, without visiting a single farm.
  • Ground truth from partnersMeasured wells from dealers and partners keep the estimates honest.
  • Better with scaleEvery installation adds a before-and-after record to learn from.
