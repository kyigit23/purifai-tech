Every drop pumped, made visible.

PURIFAI finds the farms that pump too much groundwater, then helps them switch to drip and solar irrigation with grants and 0% loans.

Talk to us See how it works

Farms use groundwater faster than it refills.

Most of it is pumped from wells nobody measures, through flood irrigation that wastes much of it. The fix exists, and the state already pays for most of it. It just isn't reaching the right farms.

  • 2.1 bn m³pumped every year beyond what the Konya basin can refill
  • 11 provincesnow under an official groundwater restriction
  • 7%of irrigated land switched to modern irrigation through grants since 2007

From a satellite view to a new irrigation system.

We work with the people farmers already trust: local irrigation and solar-pump dealers, and the banks that finance them.

  1. 1

    Detect

    Satellite imagery shows which fields are irrigated and how much water they really use, every few days.

  2. 2

    Match

    We bring those farmers to local dealers, with the grants and 0% loans that cover most of the cost.

  3. 3

    Verify

    After installation we check the field again from space. Every measured well makes our model sharper.
